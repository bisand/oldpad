A Kindle becomes an e-ink remote for lights, heating, scenes and music. You don't need to jailbreak it or install anything. It is in use on a Kindle Paperwhite 3.

![The Kindle page in black and white, with temperatures and buttons.](/img/kindle.webp)

## Set it up

1. Connect the Kindle to Wi-Fi and open its **Experimental Browser**.
2. Go to your oldpad address followed by `/kindle`, for example `https://dash.example.com/kindle`.
3. Start pairing. The page shows a QR code and a number.
4. Scan the code with your phone, sign in with your passkey, type the number and name the screen.
5. Bookmark the page.

## What to expect

- The Kindle still goes to sleep by itself, so this is a remote you wake, not an always-on display.
- The browser keeps its toolbar. oldpad fits its page below it.
- The page refreshes every five minutes while the Kindle is awake.
- It works without JavaScript. With JavaScript on, presses are quicker and spare the panel a full redraw.

## What it shows

- Up to eight info boxes: indoor, outdoor, wind, the forecast and the screen's sensors.
- Buttons for the screen's lights, sockets, thermostats and scenes. Locks and alarms stay off it.
- Music with rooms, grouping, favourites and search.
- A menu with Home, Music, Lights, Climate, Scenes, Weather and your own pages.
